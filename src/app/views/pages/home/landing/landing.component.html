Audit Management System
(AMS)

Strenghening the internal audit process through transparency,efficiency and a real-time monitoring.

A Modern Approach to Government Auditing
Key Benefits of the Audit Management System

Risk-Based Audit Plans

Execute risk profiling of audit units and prepare data driven annual audit plans to focus on critical areas

Streamlined Audit Execution

Automate daily diary maintainance ,audit para management and generate standardized reports with a clear workflow

Automated Follow-up & Compliance

Utilize a mechanism for automated follow-ups ,compliance recording and timely reminders

Insightful Reporting & Dashboards

Generate customized MIS reports and access role-based dashboards for real-time monitoring and controling

Secure Access for Authorized Personnel

This system is for authorized use by officials of the Government of Sierra Leone. All activities are monitered and recorded.
