Success metrics

ROC-AUC {{ selected.auc | number:'1.3-3' }} Chance a defaulter ranks riskier than a good payer. 0.50 is a coin toss.
Gini {{ selected.gini | number:'1.3-3' }} Same separation as ROC-AUC, on the 0 to 1 scale credit risk reports. Gini = 2 × AUC − 1.
KS {{ selected.ks | number:'1.1-1' }} Widest gap between the cumulative share of good and bad accounts.
Brier {{ selected.brier | number:'1.3-3' }} Average squared error of the probability. Lower means the predicted default rate is closer to what happened.
PSI {{ selected.psi | number:'1.2-2' }} Score mix versus training. Under 0.10 is stable. Above 0.25 is a reason to retrain.
Bad capture {{ live.recall | percent:'1.1-1' }} Share of actual defaults the cutoff still catches. This is recall on the default class.
